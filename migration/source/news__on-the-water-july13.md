# Experience the best ways to be “On the Water” – Saturday, July 13

Join Quest Watersports at [Heritage Harbor Ottawa](http://www.heritageharborottawa.com/) on Saturday, **July 13** from **11am to 2pm** to experience the BEST ways to be ON THE WATER this summer. Enjoy a relaxing day at the Heritage Harbor marina & resort, and take this opportunity to: 

- Take a ride on a [Hobie Kayak](https://www.questwatersports.com/hobie-kayaks.htm), featuring Hobie’s patented MirageDrive pedal system, which is easier & faster than paddling AND leaves your hands free to do some fishing, use a camera, or enjoy a cool drink. Learn about [kayak fishing](https://www.questwatersports.com/kayak-fishing.htm) on a [Hobie Pro Angler](https://www.questwatersports.com/2012-hobie-mirage-pro-angler--c-873.htm), the thrill of sailing a [Hobie Island](https://www.questwatersports.com/2012-hobie-mirage-tandem-island--c-882.htm), or the sheer fun of any of the [MirageDrive models](https://www.questwatersports.com/hobie-models.htm).

- Try your hand on a [stand-up paddleboard](https://www.questwatersports.com/stand-up-paddleboarding.htm), the newest and hottest way to “surf” on flat water. It’s easier than it looks, and you’ll be surprised at how much fun an on-water workout can be!

- Learn how you and your family can enjoy unlimited boating…without owning a boat. Get on-demand access to a fleet of premium [Quest Express](https://www.questwatersports.com/questexpress.htm) boats, and never worry about maintenance, cleaning, insurance, or making a boat payment!

Grab your swimwear or active clothing, some sunscreen, and expect to thoroughly enjoy your day trying one or more of these great water-based activities! RSVP to Linda at 815/433-2200, or [linda@questwatersports.com](mailto:linda@questwatersports.com).
