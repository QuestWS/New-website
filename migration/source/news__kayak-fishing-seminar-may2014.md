# Kayak Fishing & FREE Seminars – May 17

Join us on July 12 to try a Hobie Kayak & make a day of the festivities in Yorkville…all located at Bicentennial Riverfront Park & Marge Cline Whitwater Park: 

- FREE Hobie kayak tryouts from 9:30 to 5 – Hobie reps will join us & we’ll launch just upriver from the whitewater park (plenty of flat water).

- Whitewater classes & competition, cardboard regatta & whitewater rodeo from 8 to 5 – [more info here](http://www.yorkville.il.us/yorkville_fifty.php).

- Live music & Yorkville’s [Ribs on the River](http://ribsontheriver.com/) festival – all day and evening too!
