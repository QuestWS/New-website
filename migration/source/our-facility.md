# Our Facility

**Our 32,000 square foot Ottawa facility includes:**

-Exciting showroom housing our full selection of new and used boats, Hobie Kayaks & stand-up paddleboards

-Fully-equipped marine services shop

-Weather-protected drop-off area for boats in need of maintenance

-On-site, indoor warehouse to accommodate valet in/out services during summer (for boating at Heritage Harbor Ottawa), and protected storage during winter

-Convenient location approximately one mile from Heritage Harbor Ottawa and its spectacular slip, ramp, restaurant and on-water residential opportunities
