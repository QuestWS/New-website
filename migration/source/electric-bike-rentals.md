Now, experience Starved Rock Country under electric power! If you haven’t tried an electric bike, you’re in for a surprise — all the fun and freedom of your favorite bike growing up, but with a major kick! Let the built-in motor silently do the work, or add pedal power to go farther and faster than ever before.

Quest’s fleet features **Pace model eBikes from Aventon**. Choose one of five boost levels on the digital display, then simply activate the thumb throttle to get yourself moving. Add optional pedal power to accelerate faster and gain greater speed (and extend battery charge). And when it’s time to slow down, hand-operated disc brakes provide plenty of stopping power. Whatever your style, it’s easy and fun!

All rentals include a premium bike helmet; bring your own if you prefer. All bikes are equipped with a cable lock, signal bell and rear-facing safety lamp.

 RIDER REQUIREMENTS 

### Before You Ride — Please Read

Age Requirements

Riders must be at least **16 years old** and able to safely and confidently operate a bicycle. Riders under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian on their own bike.

Helmet & Footwear

A suitable bicycle **helmet must be worn at all times**. Quest provides helmets — you’re welcome to bring your own. **Open-toed shoes (sandals, flip-flops) are NOT allowed.** Comfortable sport-type shoes required.

Arrival & Check-In

Please arrive **20–30 minutes prior** to your scheduled reservation to complete paperwork, select safety equipment, and receive orientation to your eBike.

 I&M CANAL — text white on navy, image floated right 

Explore the historic I&M Canal, which is an ideal walking and biking thoroughfare through Starved Rock Country, connecting multiple state parks, the Illinois and Fox Rivers, downtown Ottawa and numerous historic sites. We highly recommend planning your eBike route around the I&M Canal trail; it is accessible directly from Heritage Harbor and is safer and more scenic than adjacent east-west roadways — [learn more about the Canal and various destinations along the way](https://www.questwatersports.com/I-and-M-canal-trail.htm). Grab a bike, jump on the path and enjoy!

When on trails, please be courteous to pedestrians and other bikers, as you will likely be moving faster than most. When passing slower-moving parties, do so where possible on their left, after first announcing that you are doing so and/or ringing your bell. When on roads, follow all legal driving and biking rules, including use of hand signals for turning.

Our eBikes are rated for a range of **up to 40 miles per charge**. This will be greatly affected by gears used, amount of pedaling vs. throttle, rider weight, and even weather conditions (wind). Remaining battery charge is indicated on the computer displays on the bikes. If a battery is expended, eBikes can be pedaled like normal bikes, but please plan your trip and monitor battery charge accordingly.
