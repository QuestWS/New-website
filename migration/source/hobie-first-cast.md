# Hobie First Cast

![](https://www.questwatersports.com/wp-content/uploads/img_1348137795.jpg)

Print

**Hobie First Cast**…it’s never been easier to experience fishing from a Hobie Kayak. Spend a few hours on a guided tour with local- and regional-level Hobie Fishing Pros to:

- Learn fishing basics from people who know where & how to catch fish locally,

- Try a Hobie Kayak on the water to get the complete kayak fishing experience, and

- Meet new people, have a great time on the water, and create some new fish stories! 

Cost is $100 per participant, which includes use of a Hobie fishing kayak, water safety equipment, and basic gear & tackle (feel free to bring your own if you like). The First Cast fee may be applied to a Hobie Kayak purchase for up to one year after your outing if desired. 

Anglers under 18 must present a signed parent/guardian liability waiver, and those under 16 must be accompanied by a particpating parent/guardian.

Keep an eye on our [homepage](https://www.questwatersports.com/index.htm) for scheduled outing dates – or let us know if you’d like to arrange a specific date for yourself or a small group. Call 815.433.2200 or [email](mailto:hobie@questwatersports.com?subject=I'd%20like%20to%20learn%20more%20about%20Hobie%20First%20Cast) for more information or to book your spot! **Fish On!**
