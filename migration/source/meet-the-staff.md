Meet the Quest Team

The people behind Quest Watersports are boaters, builders, fixers, sales pros, and customer-first problem solvers — all working together to make life on the water easier and more fun.

Jeff

Owner

Jeff has been living and breathing the water since founding Quest Watersports in 2008. He launched the region’s first membership boat club in 2011 and has spent years building Quest into a destination for marine sales, boat club membership, paddlesports, e-bikes, and more.

He’s the kind of owner who knows customers by name, welcomes people with a smile, and is always thinking about what Quest can become next.
