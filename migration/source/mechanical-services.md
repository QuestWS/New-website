# Mechanical Services

The Quest team is focused on getting you back on the water fast and at fair prices – we understand that your water time is valuable! **We never charge extra for weekend or in-water services. **

Our complete technical services include:

- Basic maintenance to complete rebuilds

- Winterization, summerization & shrink-wrapping

- Electonics/marine audio-visual installations

- Fiberglass & aluminum repair

- Canvas & vinyl repair/replacement

- Haul-out & launch (travel-lift & hydraulic trailer)

- Bottom cleaning & painting

- Pre-purchase/pre-sale inspections

- Trailer repairs

- Cleaning & detailing (a la carte and seasonal plans)

- Restorations

Our technicians are qualified to work on virtually every watercraft, motor & trailer make/model, and can provide warranty service for the brands that we carry. We stock a substantial parts inventory – and if we don’t have it, we can get it fast!

Call or see us for expert technical advice, or to schedule an appointment for in-store on in-water service.
