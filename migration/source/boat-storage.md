 INTRO 

Quest Watersports offers year-round boat storage options and off-season maintenance packages to keep your boat safe & secure. Every fall we winterize engines and onboard systems, shrinkwrap, wash and store, then bring everything back ready for the season in spring. Pick only the services you need, or let us put a package together for you.

The fastest way to see what your winter will cost is our [online winter quote](https://questws.github.io/winter-quotes_26-27/). It only asks the questions that apply to your unit, itemizes every line, and carries straight through to signing and paying your deposit.

### Engine winterization

●Basic: drain water, run on antifreeze, drain & fog, disconnect batteries

●Full service: everything in Basic plus, as applicable, engine oil & filter change, drive oil change, gimbal ring torque check, fuel conditioner and one fuel filter

 HOW IT WORKS 

How it works

### From quote to relaunch

1

Get your quote

Build it [online](https://questws.github.io/winter-quotes_26-27/): pick boat, jetski, golf cart or e-bike and answer only the questions that apply. Print it, save it, or email yourself a copy, and come back to it any time with your quote number and last name. Prefer to talk it through? Call or email us.

2

Sign your agreement online

Your storage & services agreement opens with your quote number already filled in. A completed copy is emailed to you and to Quest automatically. **[You can also sign your contract here](https://questws.github.io/winter-quotes_26-27/sign.html)**.

3

Pay your deposit

Your deposit reserves your storage space and your place in the retrieval order, scheduled in the order signed agreements and deposits come in. Pay online; cash, check, debit, Zelle and ACH carry no surcharge, and credit cards carry a 3% fee. **[Deposits and payments can be made here](https://pay.pospluslogin.com/questws).**

4

Tell us when you’re done for the season

Once your deposit is in, our emails ask when you’re ready to come out of the water: now, on a date you pick, or you’ll call. Haul-outs requested after the cutoff date on your quote carry a late retrieval surcharge.

5

We photograph it on arrival

Every unit is photographed as it arrives so you have a record of its condition, and we email you when it’s safely stored with a link to your photos.

6

Spring relaunch

Before spring we check in about any last-minute work and ask whether you’d like to launch as early as possible, any time, or as late as possible. We email when you’re up next, then send relaunch photos once you’re back in the water.

 MEASURING 

Measuring for your quote

Storage and several services are priced from your measurements, so measure the whole rig:

●Length overall (LOA): include the swim platform, outdrives or outboards, and bow pulpit. Most manufacturers leave these out of their listed length.

●On a trailer? Also measure how far the trailer sticks out past the boat, and the beam including the trailer if it’s wider.

●Jetskis: from the tip of the trailer tongue to the rearmost point, and the trailer’s full width at its widest.

Quest reserves the right to remeasure and adjust the bill accordingly.

 GOOD TO KNOW 

Good to know

● Winterization doesn’t include spring start-up. Non-stock filters, oil capacities over 5 quarts, and special OEM or synthetic lubricants are extra.

● Canvas removal, drive misalignment corrections, and generator disassembly or difficult access are billed at shop rates. Shrinkwrap removal and disposal are not included.

● Please make sure your holding tank is empty before storage.

● Let us know where your keys will be. Fees apply if a unit has to be towed out or mechanically started without keys, or needs a jump start.

● Balances not paid by the pay-by date on your quote increase, and a monthly service charge follows, as spelled out on your quote.

● Quotes are estimates based on the information you provide and are subject to Quest’s review. Storage and services are provided under Quest’s signed Storage Terms.
