# Learn About the Club

![](https://www.questwatersports.com/wp-content/uploads/img_1616137986.jpg)

 Imagine your choice of premium boats, whenever you like. Better yet, NO boat loan, NO maintenance expense or hassle, and NO storage or trailering. Just choose a boat online, pick a time to go boating, and that boat will be ready & waiting for you at the marina. Try some tubing or wakeboarding behind a nimble sport boat, or perhaps a relaxing cruise on a luxury pontoon. Bring your friends & family, enjoy a spectacular day on the water, and then drop the boat off when you’re done. All fun, no fuss…then do it again next time, all summer long! It’s like having your own fleet of boats and a personal butler to take care of the details! It’s all possible when you join Quest Express, a membership boating club based at [Heritage Harbor Ottawa](http://www.heritageharborottawa.com/). Affordable membership plans are offered, and coaching is available if needed. Quest Express Boat Club…it’s the easiest and most affordable way to boat! 

Find out more…[hear what Quest Express Members are saying](https://www.questwatersports.com/questexpress-testimonials.htm), see our [FAQs](https://www.questwatersports.com/questexpressFAQs.htm) and contact us at 815.433.2200 or by [email](mailto:boatclub@questwatersports.com?subject=I'm%20interested%20in%20learning%20more%20about%20Quest%20Express%20Boat%20Club).
