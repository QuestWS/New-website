### Fun on the water in Starved Rock Country 

 There’s something magical about being on the water; it relaxes…brings friends and family together…stirs up fun…creates memories. Water reminds us that life is an adventure. 

 At Quest Watersports we live, work and play on the water - and we’re here to help you do the same. We make your time on the water simple and carefree, and bring the latest in water-based experiences to the heart of Starved Rock Country. Let us help you discover, re-discover, or make the most of your watersports lifestyle… 

 Our original location, Riverview Marine, opened in Spring Valley, Illinois in 1992, and was recognized as the Midwest’s preferred dealer serving all types of boaters - from fishermen to high-performance enthusiasts. In 2009 we relocated to Ottawa, upgraded to a larger facility, and aligned closely with Heritage Harbor Marina, located nearby. At that time we adopted a new name as well - Quest Watersports - to reflect our growth and vision. Quest offers large and small vessels, along with a complete set of boating-related services. 

![](https://www.questwatersports.com/wp-content/themes/aanWordpress/assets/images/fun-img1.png)

![](https://www.questwatersports.com/wp-content/themes/aanWordpress/assets/images/fun-img2.png)

 In 2011 Quest Watersports launched Quest Express Boat Club, the first such program in the state. Quest Express allows unlimited access to a fleet of club boats on an annual membership basis - and eliminates the need for members to purchase, maintain, transport, store and insure their own boat. At that time Quest also added Hobie Kayaks to its lineup, as well as a premium paddlesports rental program. 

 Keep an eye on Quest as we bring you even more ways to have fun on the water! Whether you’re “sticking your toe in the water” for the first time - or are a seasoned watersports enthusiast - Quest can help make your waterborne adventures the best they can be…
