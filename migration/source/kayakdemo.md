# Find Your Perfect Kayak at Quest Watersports!

Ever wondered what it feels like to pedal across the water with the patented Hobie Mirage Drive or Old Town PDL Drive? Or maybe you’ve been curious about the Minn Kota powered Old Town Sportsman kayaks? At Quest Watersports, you can experience these for **FREE**!

Take a demo ride for a hands-on journey with a wide array Hobie and Old Town models. Dive into the serenity of the breathtaking Heritage Harbor, a 32-acre marina, just 1.5 miles away from the Quest Showroom.

## Why Opt for a Demo Ride at Quest Watersports?

- Test various Hobie and Old Town models at no cost

- Experience the pedal and paddle sensation in the picturesque Heritage Harbor

- Receive expert guidance to help you select the perfect kayak

- Easily book your test ride over the phone or through our website

Whether you’re a beginner keen to explore or a seasoned enthusiast eager to upgrade, Quest Watersports is committed to guiding you in choosing the right kayak for your adventures, and to schedule a test ride at a time that fits into your plans.
